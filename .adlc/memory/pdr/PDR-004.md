## PDR-004: Commercial Intent: Free Open Source

### Status

**Completed**

### Date

2026-08-23

### Owner

### Category

Business Model

### Feature-Area

system

### Context
**Problem/Opportunity:**
The spec defined features but no commercial intent. Intent gates persona depth, onboarding polish, support burden, and license strategy.

**Market Forces:**
- Self-host/BYOK culture strongly favors open tooling (n8n fair-code precedent)
- Trust is the primary adoption criterion for mail-reading software

### Decision
**Decision Statement:**
Release as free open source under the **MIT license** (finalized 2026-08-23). Community-driven; no paid tier in v1.

**Rationale:**
Adoption and trust first; contributions offset maintenance; consistent with the target persona's values and the category's norms.

### Consequences
#### Positive
- Zero-friction adoption
- Community contributions and bug reports
- License builds trust for an app that reads mail

#### Negative
- No direct revenue
- Maintenance obligation and feature-request pressure begin at launch

#### Risks
- Perceived abandonment → public roadmap and steady changelog cadence
- Brand misuse after traction → define governance before publicity push

### Alternatives Considered
#### Option C: Open-core
**Description:** Free core + paid premium tier later.
**Trade-offs:** Revenue path without betraying local-first, but split-brain feature planning.

#### Option D: Paid indie app
**Description:** One-time license (~$50–80).
**Trade-offs:** Simplest revenue; harder launch without existing audience.
